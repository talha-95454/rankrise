    💡 Untapped keyword ideas

    These are rule-based ideas — phrases only your own listing uses, and new combinations of your app's title phrases — that none of the tracked competitors are using. There is no search-volume data behind them, so use “Check competition” to see how strong the current top apps for a phrase are before betting a title slot on it.
